### 📱 Nabor — On-Demand Services Marketplace

**Connecting people with trusted local service professionals.**

The platform aims to connect customers with verified professionals including plumbers, electricians, mechanics, barbers, and other service providers.

**Planned capabilities:**

- Verified service provider profiles

- In-app communication
- Digital quotations and secure payments
- Customer ratings and reviews
- Provider management and earnings dashboards
- Centralized administration and dispute management

**Platforms:** Web | Android | iOS

**Status:** In Development

## 🧩 My Engineering Philosophy

> "Good architecture isn't about adding complexity. It's about making deliberate decisions that balance business value, simplicity, scalability, and long-term maintainability."

I approach software engineering with a focus on:

**Business Value First** — Technology should solve meaningful problems.

**Architecture with Purpose** — Every technical decision should have a clear rationale.

**Scalability by Design** — Systems should evolve as business requirements change.

**Security and Reliability** — These are foundational engineering concerns, not afterthoughts.

**Continuous Improvement** — Great engineering comes from learning, collaboration, and iteration.
